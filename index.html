How to turn the live view into a candidate list

Prices and calendar update externally; structure and liquidity still need your own rules.

Decision support
1Filter the environmentCheck whether the market is open, the XM spread is normal, and a relevant high-impact release is near.
2Set the contextUse Daily/H4 to classify trend or range; record important swing points and prior day/week levels.
3Wait for evidenceAt a meaningful level, watch for sweep/reclaim or clean continuation, then a structure shift and your defined trigger.
4Verify in XMConfirm the same level, current bid/ask, spread, contract value and stop distance inside the XM MT5 app before any decision.
