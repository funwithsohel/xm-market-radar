One repeatable scan

Use the score to organize your review—not as a win probability. A liquidity sweep is useful evidence, but it is not mandatory for every continuation setup.

1Calendar & sessionMark relevant events and time in Bahrain time (UTC+3). Check whether the instrument is open and its spread is acceptable.
2Higher-timeframe contextStart with Daily/H4 direction or range; note the swing structure and where price is in that structure.
3Liquidity locationRecord a meaningful level: prior day/week high or low, session extreme, equal highs/lows, or a clear swing.
4Wait for price actionLook for a sweep/reclaim or a clean continuation, then a structure shift and your chosen trigger. H1 setup → M15 trigger; adapt for swing trades.
5Define the riskWrite the invalidation before entry. Use the XM symbol’s own tick value, contract size and trading conditions to size any position.

Symbol-specific context from your list

Metals, USD and energy

Gold and silver can react to USD moves, yields, central-bank communication and risk sentiment; oil has its own supply, inventory and demand catalysts. Treat these as context, not a directional rule.

Forex pairs

Check the calendar for both currencies in a pair. Crosses such as GBPJPY and GBPCHF can move on either side’s central-bank news and broader risk sentiment.

Indices, crypto and equity CFD

Check each symbol’s trading hours and spread. Index moves may react to macro news and constituent earnings; crypto can be volatile around the clock; single-share CFDs can gap on company headlines.

Two USD symbols to distinguish

USD_FX_Index and USDX-DEC26 are separate symbols in your list. Confirm how XM quotes each one. The latter is labelled December 2026, so check its contract dates, hours and roll conditions in the XM specifications.
